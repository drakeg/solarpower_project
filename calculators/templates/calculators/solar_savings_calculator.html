UNDERSTAND THE ESTIMATE

What this compares

  1. Current costs. Your monthly bill is multiplied by 12 to estimate annual spending.
  2. System lifetime. The entered solar cost is spread over the expected number of years for an annualized comparison.
  3. Not a full financial model. This tool does not yet calculate financing, incentives, maintenance, or solar generation.
