ENERGY BASICS

Watts vs. watt-hours

  1. Watts (W) measure instantaneous power draw.
  2. Watt-hours (Wh) measure energy used over time: watts × hours × quantity.
  3. Kilowatt-hours (kWh) are 1,000 Wh. For a complete system, add multiple appliances to a worksheet.
