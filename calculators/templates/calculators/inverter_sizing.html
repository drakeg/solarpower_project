BEFORE YOU BEGIN

Plan for peak demand

  1. Simultaneous loads. Count devices that may run at the same time, not every device you own.
  2. Startup surge. Motors and compressors often draw more power when starting.
  3. Check the label. Verify both continuous and surge ratings, including how long the inverter can sustain the surge.
